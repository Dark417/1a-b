# 07 · Google models — Chinchilla, PaLM, Gemini, Gemma

> **Intuition.** Google/DeepMind contributed two things that shape the whole
> field: the **scaling law that told everyone how to spend compute** (Chinchilla),
> and a lineage of frontier models (PaLM → Gemini) plus an open small-model
> family (Gemma). The throughline is *compute-optimality* and engineering at
> Pathways/TPU scale.

---

## 1. Chinchilla (DeepMind, 2022) — the scaling law that mattered

Kaplan et al. (2020, OpenAI) found loss is a power law in params, data, and
compute, but **over-weighted model size**. DeepMind re-ran the experiments and
found that for a fixed compute budget $C \approx 6ND$ (N params, D tokens), the
**compute-optimal** point scales **N and D roughly equally** — about
**~20 training tokens per parameter**.

The demonstration: **Chinchilla (70B, 1.4T tokens)** beat **Gopher (280B, 300B
tokens)** despite being 4× smaller — because Gopher was badly *under-trained*.
Takeaway that reshaped the industry: don't just grow the model, **feed it more
data**. (LLaMA then pushed *past* compute-optimal on purpose for cheap inference —
see `04.llama-line.md`, `11.training-and-alignment.md`.)

$$N_{\text{opt}} \propto C^{0.5},\qquad D_{\text{opt}} \propto C^{0.5},\qquad D_{\text{opt}}/N_{\text{opt}} \approx 20$$

## 2. PaLM (Google, 2022) — Pathways Language Model

A **540B** dense decoder trained across two TPU v4 pods via the **Pathways**
system. Architectural choices that later spread widely:
- **SwiGLU** FFN activations (`05.swiglu.py`).
- **Parallel** attention + FFN layers (compute the two sub-blocks from the same
  input in parallel — faster at scale).
- **Multi-Query Attention** (`03.gqa_mqa.py`) for fast decoding.
- RoPE (`01.rope.py`), no biases, shared input/output embeddings.

PaLM showed strong emergent reasoning (chain-of-thought, BIG-bench). **PaLM 2**
(2023) followed; **Med-PaLM**, **PaLM-E** (embodied/multimodal) are branches.

---

## 3. Gemini (Google DeepMind, 2023→2025) — natively multimodal frontier

Gemini is **multimodal from the ground up** (text, image, audio, video tokens in
a single model) rather than stitching a vision encoder onto an LLM. Notable:
**Gemini 1.5 Pro** introduced a **very long context (up to 1M, demoed to 2M
tokens)**, widely understood to use a **sparse MoE** architecture **(inferred —
Google has not disclosed full specifics)**. Gemini 2.x continues the line with
"thinking"/reasoning variants. Treat exact sizes and layer counts as
**undisclosed/inferred**.

---

## 4. Gemma (Google, 2024) — the open small models

Built from the Gemini research/tech, released as **open weights** under the Gemma
license.

### Gemma-1 (Feb 2024, 2B/7B)
Decoder with RoPE, RMSNorm, **GeGLU** activations (GELU-gated GLU, cousin of
SwiGLU — `05.swiglu.py`), a **large ~256k vocabulary**, and **MQA on 2B / MHA on
7B**. 8k context.

### Gemma-2 (June 2024, 2B/9B/27B)
Two training/architecture ideas worth knowing:
- **Knowledge distillation.** The 2B and 9B are trained to match the **output
  distribution of a larger 27B teacher** (not just next-token labels), giving
  outsized quality for their size. (Distillation also appears in `01.encoder-models.md`
  for DistilBERT.)
- **Logit soft-capping.** Bound logits into a fixed range via
  $\text{cap}\cdot\tanh(z/\text{cap})$ to stabilize training (soft-cap ≈ 50 for
  attention logits, ≈ 30 for the final layer).
- **Alternating local (sliding-window) and global attention** layers
  (`09.sliding_window.py`) — most layers are cheap local, some are global —
  plus GQA. Pre- **and** post-norm RMSNorm around each sub-block.

**Gemma-3** (2025) adds longer context and vision variants on the same lineage.

---

## 5. Strengths / limits

- **Chinchilla** is a *law*, not a model: its value is the recipe. Caveat: it
  ignores inference cost, which is why deployment-oriented models over-train.
- **PaLM/Gemini** are closed (API/weights), so much detail is inferred; their
  engineering (Pathways, MoE, long context, multimodality) set frontier bars.
- **Gemma** is the practical, open, well-engineered small-model family; the
  soft-capping and local/global mix are the distinctive bits.
