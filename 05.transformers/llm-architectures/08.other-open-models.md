# 08 · Other open models — NeoX, OPT, BLOOM, Pythia, MPT, Falcon, Phi, Yi, StableLM, Command-R, DBRX, Grok, OLMo

> **Intuition.** Beyond the headline families, a wide field of open models each
> contributed one or two ideas: a reproducible GPT-3, a fully-open *science*
> suite, ALiBi long context, multi-query attention at scale, the "small model on
> great synthetic data" thesis, retrieval/tool tuning, fine-grained MoE, and
> radical openness (data + code + logs). This file is the field guide.

---

## 1. The early open large models (2022)

- **GPT-NeoX-20B (EleutherAI).** One of the first open ~20B models. Decoder with
  **RoPE** (`01.rope.py`) and **parallel attention/FFN** layers; trained on **The
  Pile**. The codebase (`gpt-neox`) trained many later models. Apache-2.0.
- **OPT (Meta, 125M–175B).** An **open reproduction of GPT-3**, shipped with a
  candid training **logbook** documenting instabilities — a milestone in
  open/transparent LLM science. Research license.
- **BLOOM (BigScience, 176B).** Community-built, **multilingual (46 natural + 13
  programming languages)**, uses **ALiBi** positions (`02.alibi.py`). Released
  under the **RAIL** responsible-AI license. The largest fully-documented open
  multilingual model of its era.

## 2. The science/interpretability suite

- **Pythia (EleutherAI, 70M–12B).** A *suite* of 8 sizes trained on the **same
  data in the same order**, with **154 checkpoints each** released. Built for
  **interpretability and training-dynamics research** (memorization, scaling,
  bias over training). Apache-2.0. Not chasing SOTA — chasing *reproducibility*.

## 3. Long-context and efficient open models

- **MPT (MosaicML, 7B/30B).** Commercial-friendly (Apache-2.0), **ALiBi**
  (`02.alibi.py`) for length extrapolation (MPT-7B-StoryWriter ran 65k+ context),
  FlashAttention (`10.flash_attention.py`) training. Proved you could train a
  strong open model cheaply and ship it for commercial use.
- **Falcon (TII, 7B/40B/180B).** **Multi-query attention** (`03.gqa_mqa.py`),
  parallel attention/FFN, and the high-quality **RefinedWeb** dataset (showing
  well-filtered web data rivals curated corpora). Apache-2.0.

## 4. Small-but-mighty: the Phi line (Microsoft)

- **Phi-1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 (1.3B–14B).** The **"Textbooks Are All You Need"**
  thesis: train small models on **high-quality, partly synthetic, textbook-like
  data** and they punch far above their weight (Phi-2 2.7B rivaling much larger
  models; Phi-3-mini 3.8B with 128k context). **MIT** licensed. Demonstrates
  **data quality > raw scale** at small sizes. (Caveat: heavy reliance on
  synthetic/benchmark-adjacent data drew scrutiny about contamination.)

## 5. Bilingual and general open models

- **Yi (01.AI, 6B/34B).** Strong **bilingual EN/ZH**, long-context variants up to
  200k, LLaMA-style architecture. Apache-2.0.
- **StableLM (Stability AI, 1.6B–12B).** A general open family; later versions
  adopt GQA/RoPE. Mixed custom/Apache licensing.
- **OLMo / OLMo-2 (Allen Institute for AI, 1B–13B).** **Fully open**: not just
  weights but the **training data (Dolma), code, logs, and every checkpoint**.
  The gold standard for *reproducible* open science models. Apache-2.0.

## 6. Retrieval/tool-tuned and MoE open models

- **Command-R / Command-R+ (Cohere, 35B / 104B).** Purpose-built for **RAG and
  tool use**: trained to ground answers in retrieved documents with citations and
  to call tools. **128k** context, GQA. CC-BY-NC (research) weights.
- **DBRX (Databricks, 132B total / 36B active).** A **fine-grained MoE**:
  **16 experts, top-4** chosen (vs Mixtral/Grok's 8-choose-2), giving 65× more
  routing combinations. Trained on 12T tokens. Databricks Open license.
- **Grok-1 (xAI, 314B total / ~86B active).** An **8-expert top-2 MoE**; base
  weights released under **Apache-2.0** (a large open MoE drop). Later Grok
  versions are closed/API.

## 7. One-line "what it taught us" table

| Model | The lesson |
|---|---|
| GPT-NeoX-20B | open training stack + RoPE + parallel layers |
| OPT | reproduce GPT-3 *transparently* (logbook) |
| BLOOM | massively multilingual, ALiBi, RAIL license |
| Pythia | reproducible suite for interpretability science |
| MPT | cheap, commercial, ALiBi long context |
| Falcon | well-filtered web data + MQA scales |
| Phi | small model + great (synthetic) data wins |
| Yi | strong bilingual + long context, open |
| StableLM | broad open family |
| Command-R+ | RAG/tool-use as a first-class training target |
| DBRX | fine-grained MoE (16×top-4) |
| Grok-1 | a large open MoE release |
| OLMo | *fully* open (data+code+logs+checkpoints) |
