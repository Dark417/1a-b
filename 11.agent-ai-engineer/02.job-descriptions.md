# 02 · Condensed Job-Description Corpus

A condensed, **paraphrased** corpus of the roles behind the ranking. Each entry
is a few lines: **company · role · level · key required skills · nice-to-haves ·
source**. This is **not verbatim JD text** — requirements are extracted and
summarized (per the house citation rules and to respect copyright). Source IDs
(S#/R#) map to [`01.research-method.md`](01.research-method.md).

Legend for level: NG = new-grad/junior · Mid · Sr = senior · Staff/Principal ·
Research = research-track. "Req" = required, "Nice" = preferred/plus.

## Cross-cutting observations from the corpus

- **SWE foundation is universal and listed first.** Almost every role — even
  research and FDE — leads with "strong software engineer who happens to do AI,"
  not the reverse (`S1`, `S3`, `S6`, `S8`, `R9`). *Industry-knowledge note: this
  is consistent across the broader market; senior JDs assume system design,
  testing, and debugging even when terse — flagged here as inferred where a JD
  was thin (`S14`, `S18`).*
- **Python is non-negotiable; a second language separates seniors.** Go/C++ at
  OpenAI (`S1`), C/C++/CUDA at NVIDIA (`S9`), TS for agent/FDE work (`S4`, `S30`).
- **The 2025–2026 "GenAI stack" is now table stakes for applied roles:**
  prompt engineering + RAG + agents + evaluation + vector DBs recur together
  across `S2`–`S5`, `S15`–`S30` and the startup aggregate.
- **Evaluation is rising into a first-class skill** (`S3`, `S5`, `S16`, `S29`,
  `S30`) — own role at Scale, named requirement at Anthropic, central to agentic
  work.
- **Specialist depth (CUDA/distributed/inference) pays and gates specific roles**
  (`S9`, `S10`, `S27`, `S28`) but is *not* asked of most applied AI-engineer jobs.
- **Hiring on evidence over credentials** is explicit at labs and startups
  (`S3`/`R10`, `S21`, `S22`): repos, deployed agents, eval suites, blog posts.
